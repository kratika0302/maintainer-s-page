Maintainer events

You don't have to figure this out alone.

A maintainer's hardest problem usually isn't code. It's not knowing anyone else stuck on the same thing. These are the events built specifically to fix that.

Annual

Maintainer Summit

A day of maintainers talking to maintainers — held the day before IndiaFOSS. No pitches, no sponsors on stage, just people comparing notes on governance, burnout, funding and the parts of the job nobody warns you about.

Details on fossunited.org →
Annual

IndiaFOSS

India's largest FOSS conference, and the anchor event for the whole community calendar. Maintainer Programs runs its visibility campaigns, grant announcements and community meetups around it every year.

Details on fossunited.org →
Monthly

Maintainer's May

A month to come together as keepers of the digital commons. Vent about the good, the bad. Rally ideas and resources.

Details on fossunited.org →
Monthly

Maintainer Community Calls

A standing call for anyone in the Community or Support layer of the program — not a webinar, a working session where maintainers bring what they're stuck on.

Local & Partner Meetups
